Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I close?
+
We typically close in 14–21 business days from the time you accept our offer. In some cases, we can move even faster. You also choose the closing date — we work on your timeline.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?
+
Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition — fire damage, water damage, structural issues, code violations, hoarder properties, you name it. Don't spend a single dollar on repairs before calling us.
Are there any fees or commissions?
+
Zero. We are not real estate agents. There are no commissions, no closing costs charged to you, and no hidden fees. The cash offer we give you is what you walk away with.
What types of homes do you buy?
+
We buy all types of residential properties — single family homes, multi-family, condos, townhomes, and even mobile homes in some cases. Vacant, occupied, distressed, inherited — all of it.
Is your cash offer fair?
+
We make offers based on the current market value of your home, its condition, and comparable sales in the area. Our offers are fair and transparent — we'll always explain exactly how we arrived at the number.
What areas of Georgia do you serve?
+
We are based in Atlanta and serve homeowners throughout the entire state of Georgia — including metro Atlanta, Savannah, Augusta, Columbus, Macon, and everywhere in between.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?
+
Never. Our cash offer is 100% free and comes with zero obligation. If our offer doesn't work for you, there's no pressure and no hard feelings. We're here to help, not to push.
