Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in full transparency. Here are the questions we hear most often.

How fast can I close?
+
We typically close in 14 to 21 business days — sometimes faster depending on your situation. Unlike traditional sales that can take 60–90 days, we move at your pace and can often accommodate urgent timelines.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?
+
Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition — fire damage, foundation issues, code violations, storm damage, or simply a house that needs updating. You don't need to fix a single thing before selling to us.
Are there any fees or commissions?
+
Zero. We cover all closing costs and there are no realtor commissions. The offer we make you is the amount you walk away with. No hidden fees, no surprises at closing.
What types of properties do you buy?
+
We buy single-family homes, multi-family properties, vacant land, distressed properties, inherited homes, foreclosures, and more — anywhere in Georgia. If you're unsure, just call us and we'll let you know.
Is my offer really fair?
+
We base our offers on current market conditions, the property's location, and its condition. We're investors — not traditional buyers — so our offer reflects that. But we're transparent about how we calculate it and will always explain our reasoning.
What if I'm behind on payments or in foreclosure?
+
Time is critical in foreclosure situations. Contact us immediately — we can often close fast enough to help you avoid foreclosure, protect your credit, and potentially walk away with cash. Don't wait.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?
+
Never. Our offers are 100% no-obligation. You can request an offer, review it, and walk away with no pressure and no hard feelings. We want you to feel confident in your decision.
